The Process

Four steps. One clear operating picture.

Client answers the 24 controls based on how the household or organization operates today.

02

Responses become a preliminary score and reveal weak controls, patterns, and evidence gaps.

Six Domains

The whole readiness picture.

The method evaluates practices that influence whether clients can prevent, withstand, and recover from common security and continuity problems.

01

Identity & Access

MFA, password management, recovery methods, and privileged access.

02

Device Security

Updates, endpoint protection, encryption, and unsupported technology.

03

Network & Wi-Fi

Wireless security, router maintenance, segmentation, and exposure.

04

Data & Backups

Backup coverage, protected copies, restore testing, and sensitive records.

05

Fraud & Social Engineering

Phishing awareness, verification, public exposure, and reporting.

06

Preparedness & Continuity

Offline access, outages, incident response, and remediation tracking.

Assessment Boundary

Clear scope. No false promises.

This is a defensive readiness baseline and professional advisory review. It does not perform exploitation or penetration testing, certify legal or regulatory compliance, provide legal advice, or guarantee that a person or organization will not experience a security incident.
