Privacy & Data Handling

CLIENT INFORMATION DESERVES DISCIPLINE.

Launch-site data handling

This public launch site is intentionally static and does not maintain a public client account system or assessment database.

Service requests

The service-request form prepares an email in the visitor’s own email application. The site itself does not submit the form to a FlintWatch public web database.

Do not send secrets

Do not provide passwords, recovery codes, private keys, API keys, Social Security numbers, payment-card numbers, or other authentication secrets through this website, questionnaire comments, or service-request email.

Assessment information

Questionnaire responses are used to support readiness assessment, professional review, and recommendations. Self-reported results remain unverified until FlintWatch completes professional review.

Contact

Questions can be sent to flintwatch.command@gmail.com.
