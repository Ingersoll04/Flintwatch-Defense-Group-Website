Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before we begin.

Can FlintWatch combine multiple services into one engagement?

Yes. Integrated engagements can combine cyber readiness, preparedness, continuity, selected physical-security planning, policy, and training recommendations when they fit the lawful scope of the engagement.

Do you work with small businesses and families?

Yes. FlintWatch is specifically structured to make professional security guidance practical for small organizations, families, and community groups.

Is the Security & Readiness Assessment a penetration test or compliance certification?

No. It is a defensive readiness baseline and professional advisory review. It does not perform exploitation or penetration testing, provide legal advice, certify compliance, or guarantee security.

Where does FlintWatch operate?

FlintWatch is based in Arizona and is initially focused on serving clients throughout Southern Arizona. Expanded service areas can be discussed during intake.
