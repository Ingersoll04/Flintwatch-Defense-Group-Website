Service Scope

CLEAR BOUNDARIES BUILD TRUST.

Current launch services

FlintWatch’s current public launch focuses on Security & Readiness Assessments, cyber-readiness advisory, preparedness/training support, and risk review/planning.

Protective Services

Protective Services is a later-stage regulated capability. FlintWatch does not represent guard/security or other regulated protective services as active unless and until applicable licensing, insurance, policy, and compliance requirements are satisfied.

Assessment boundary

The Security & Readiness Assessment is a defensive readiness baseline and professional advisory review. It is not penetration testing, a vulnerability exploitation engagement, legal advice, regulatory/compliance certification, emergency response, or a guarantee of security.

Professional review

Client questionnaire results begin as self-reported and unverified. FlintWatch may confirm, raise, lower, or exclude individual scores after review when support exists. The client’s original historical answers remain preserved.

Engagement-specific terms

Any written proposal or agreement for a specific engagement should define exact deliverables, exclusions, fees, schedule, responsibilities, and applicable limitations.
